Plan Your Income for a Predictable Budget

Understand the importance of income planning in your monthly budget and how to set your expected earnings in Budget Plan.

September 07, 2026 | budgeting | privacy | habits

Your monthly budget is a roadmap for your money, and a crucial part of that roadmap is knowing your expected income. By taking a moment to plan how much you anticipate earning, you lay the foundation for a more stable and less stressful financial month. This screen in Budget Plan helps you do just that.

Why Income Planning Matters

When you set a clear expectation for your monthly earnings, you empower yourself to make informed decisions about your spending. Without this foresight, it's easy to overspend or feel uncertain about where your money is going. Knowing your expected income allows you to allocate funds more realistically to categories like rent, groceries, transit, and even savings goals. It’s the first step in creating a budget that works for you, rather than one that feels restrictive or unattainable.

For instance, if you consistently earn around €2500 per month, inputting this figure helps you understand the total amount available to be budgeted. This number then becomes the ceiling for your expenses, guiding you to plan your spending within your means. It’s about creating a clear picture of your financial inflow before you start planning your outflow.

Setting Your Expected Income

The screen prompts you with a simple question: "What do you expect to earn this month?" Below this, it clarifies, "How much do you expect to earn this month?" This straightforward approach makes it easy to input your anticipated income.

You'll see a field showing a dollar sign followed by a number, which you can edit using the numerical keypad provided. For example, if you expect to earn €2500, you would simply type that amount. The keypad allows for precise entry, including decimal points if your income is not a round figure.

Once you've entered your expected income, a "CONTINUE" button is available at the bottom of the screen. This indicates that this is the first step in a process, and your input here will be used in subsequent steps to build your comprehensive monthly budget.

Your Next Step: Estimate Your Income

Taking control of your finances starts with understanding your income. For your next budgeting action today, open Budget Plan and navigate to the income planning screen. Enter the amount you realistically expect to earn this month. This simple act is a powerful step towards a calmer, more organised financial life.
